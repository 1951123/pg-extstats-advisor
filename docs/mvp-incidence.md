# MVP conservative incidence

The PG16.14 source audit uses `src/backend/statistics/extended_stats.c`: `statext_is_compatible_clause_internal` (around 1325--1570), `statext_is_compatible_clause` (1572--1650), and `statext_mcv_clauselist_selectivity` (1696--1800). FD checks are in `src/backend/statistics/dependencies.c`: `dependency_is_compatible_clause` (741--875) and `dependencies_clauselist_selectivity` (1352--1470). These paths restrict compatible clauses to one relation, supported variable/operator forms, and select statistics by matched attributes; extended statistics need multi-column evidence. Expressions and inherited-statistics nuances remain outside this MVP.

For a precisely inspected query, the artifact adds an MCV or FD edge when candidate and query target the same relation and at least two candidate attributes have supported predicates. This includes an arity-three candidate used through two matching attributes and therefore does not require every candidate column. For any ambiguous/unclassified predicate, every candidate on the target relation receives an edge (`conservative-fallback`). False positives reduce reuse but cannot change estimates; within the declared simple-predicate scope, same-relation candidates that can supply multi-column evidence are not omitted.

`incidence.json` binds workload and full candidate-catalog digests, derivation version, sorted candidate/query edges and their `precise-structural` or `conservative-fallback` reasons, edge count, fallback-query count, and artifact digest. Unknown candidates/queries remain rejected by `IncidenceIndex`. Unsupported structural query shapes fail during ingestion rather than receiving speculative edges.
